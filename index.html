⛏️ Minecraft Materials Checklist

Tick items off as you gather them. Progress is saved in your browser.
Raw amounts assume: stonecutter for stone, copper and slabs/stairs/walls; mossy blocks made with vines (swap for moss blocks if you like); wood counts are logs (1 log = 4 planks); iron nuggets counted as ingots (9 nuggets = 1 ingot). Buckets, lanterns etc. may be counted once per placement in the video — if you only need to reuse a few buckets, cut the iron down.
